## Why the old bound (r.ms >= 6500 from run start) could fail
scripts/test.sh counts the time limit in whole seconds (date +%s). With a 2 s limit, TERM comes
1 to 2 s after the start, not at 2.0 s. So total = TERM offset (1-2 s, plus startup) + about 5.3 s wait.
A short offset gives a total near 6.3 s. Data below (total - term_to_end = TERM offset):
run 5 of item 1 had total 6761 ms with a 1244 ms offset; the others had 2.2-2.4 s offsets. The failed run
(6354 ms) fits an offset near 1.0-1.1 s. The wait itself is stable (term_to_end 5400-5517 ms).
The lower bound was a property of the second boundary, not of machine load. The test.sh code has no defect.
